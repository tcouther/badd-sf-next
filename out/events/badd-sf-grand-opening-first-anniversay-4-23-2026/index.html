Celebrate BADD’s 1st Anniversary And Grand Opening

Thursday, April 23rd, 2026
233 Eddy Street, San Francisco
12:00pm to 3:00pm

Dear Community Members, Friends, and Supporters,

On behalf of everyone at BADD Brothers Against Drug Deaths, we extend our deepest gratitude to each of you who joined us on April 23, 2026, for our Grand Opening and 1st Anniversary celebration at our new home on Eddy Street in San Francisco.

Your presence made this milestone truly meaningful. What began as a deeply personal mission has grown into a movement rooted in compassion, advocacy, and life-saving support. Gathering together to officially open our doors and mark one year of impact was a powerful reminder that none of this work happens alone—it happens because of community.




We were honored to share this special day with individuals, families, partners, and allies who believe in our vision: turning pain into purpose and creating pathways to healing for those affected by substance use and mental health challenges. Your energy, encouragement, and commitment continue to fuel our work and strengthen our resolve.

This event was more than a celebration—it was a reaffirmation of why we do what we do. Every conversation, every connection, and every show of support helps us move closer to a future where fewer lives are lost and more lives are restored.

As we look ahead, we remain committed to expanding our reach, deepening our services, and continuing to stand alongside the communities we serve. We invite you to stay engaged, stay connected, and continue this journey with us.

Thank you for standing with BADD. Thank you for believing in the mission. And most importantly, thank you for being part of this growing family.

With gratitude and purpose,

BADD Brothers Against Drug Deaths “From Pain to Purpose”
